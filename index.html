All 3×3 figure doors

We're building every 3×3 figure door there is, all 512 of them.

A figure door doesn't close flat. Some of its 9 blocks stop one block behind the others, and the closed door shows a figure.

1 in front 0 one block behind

Each door is built on synergyserver.net and then moves to the showcase plot, next to the others. Anyone from the server is welcome to build one. Free figures are on the , or in game: /warp tp figure

  1. Pick a figure nobody has taken yet, on the or in game: /warp tp figure
  2. Build the door on synergyserver.net. The are short, have a look first.
  3. Choose an item for the wireless input. The ones already taken are listed in the sheet.
  4. Send the coords and the item on Discord or in game: /mail send nesclass

That's it. Your door goes on the map and later moves to the showcase plot.

Must

  • The door fits in a 13×13×2 box, with the 3×3 right in the middle of it.
  • It's seamless from the front. can have seams and gaps.
  • It's mobless, non-locational and non-directional.
  • The figure is made of quartz blocks.
  • The input is a lever or a button.
  • It doesn't take too long to open or close.

Even better

  • It's layout-sized. Bigger is fine when you're going for something funny or a challenge: 0-tick, obsless, beta, cracked, complex animation and so on.
  • It's NTTless.
  • The input has its own block, on the front.

Don't repeat yourself

  • You can build up to 16 doors.
  • Try to make each one different. Up to 8 of yours can share a layout.
  • A figure and its inverse need different wiring. Reusing it is cheating.
