All 3×3 figure doors

A 3×3 door has nine cells. Fill some, leave the rest empty, and you get one of 512 figures. This project is about building a piston door for each of them.

A door closes into its figure: 101 111 101 is an H, for example.

Finished doors move to one showcase plot on synergyserver.net, all 512 side by side. Anyone on the server can join in: pick a free figure and build its door. Who builds what is tracked in the sheet and at the reference plot: /warp tp figure

  1. Grab a free figure from the , or see them all in game: /warp tp figure
  2. Build the door on synergyserver.net. Rules are in the next tab.
  3. Pick an item for the wireless input that isn't in the sheet yet.
  4. Send the coords and the item on Discord or in game: /mail send nesclass

Must

  • 13×13×2 max, opening in the centre.
  • Seamless and mobless.
  • Non-locational, non-directional.
  • Quartz block for the figure.
  • A lever or a button for the input.
  • Doesn't take too long to open or close.

Even better

  • Fits in 11×11×2.
  • NTTless.
  • Input on its own block, on the front.

Don't repeat yourself

  • 16 doors per person at most.
  • Try to build every door differently. Up to 8 of yours can share a layout.
  • A figure and its inverse need different wiring. Reusing it is cheating.
